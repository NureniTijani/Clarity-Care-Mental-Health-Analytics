## 🪄 **Overview**

The **Clarity Care Suicidal Data Analysis** project explores critical mental health insights through comprehensive data analysis using **Microsoft Power BI**.  

This project focuses on identifying **patterns, demographic trends, and risk factors associated with suicide cases**, leveraging data visualization to support awareness, prevention, and informed decision-making.  

By analyzing the provided dataset, this Power BI dashboard delivers an evidence-based approach to understanding the complex interplay between **age, gender, regional distribution, and contributing causes**, empowering stakeholders in the mental health and public health sectors with actionable insights.

## 📈 **Dashboard Insights**

The **Clarity Care Power BI Dashboard** consolidates complex data into interactive, easy-to-understand visuals, highlighting:

- **Suicide Rates by Age Group and Gender**  
- **Regional Distribution of Cases**  
- **Yearly and Monthly Suicide Trends**  
- **Primary Causes and Risk Factors**  
- **Socioeconomic and Demographic Correlations**  

These insights provide a holistic view of the data, helping policymakers, researchers, and healthcare professionals design targeted intervention strategies.  

---

## 🔍 **Key Analyses**

### 👥 **Demographic Distribution**
- **Gender Trends:** Males represent a higher percentage of suicide cases, consistent with global mental health data.  
- **Age Group Analysis:** The highest occurrences are among **young adults and middle-aged individuals**, suggesting critical target groups for intervention.  
- **Insight:** Tailored awareness campaigns and mental health outreach programs are essential for at-risk age brackets.

---

### 🌍 **Regional and Temporal Analysis**
- **Top Affected Regions:** Certain regions report consistently higher case numbers, potentially linked to socioeconomic stress or access to care disparities.  
- **Temporal Trends:** Suicide cases show distinct seasonal and yearly variations.  
- **Insight:** Regional policy and resource allocation should be data-informed to mitigate rising trends in vulnerable areas.

---

### 💬 **Contributing Factors**
- **Primary Causes:** Commonly reported triggers include **mental illness, financial hardship, relationship issues, and unemployment**.  
- **Correlations:** Economic downturns and social isolation indicators correspond with increased case frequencies.  
- **Insight:** Early intervention programs and community-based mental health support are crucial preventive measures.  

---

### 📊 **Yearly Progression**
- **Longitudinal Trends:** The dashboard reveals a fluctuation in suicide rates across multiple years, reflecting the impact of economic, social, and environmental factors.  
- **Insight:** Data-driven policymaking is necessary for understanding shifts and measuring the impact of awareness initiatives.  

---

## 💡 **Recommendations**

### **1. Awareness & Prevention Programs**
- Implement **targeted campaigns** focused on youth and middle-aged adults.  
- Leverage schools, workplaces, and media platforms to promote open discussions about mental health.  

### **2. Accessibility of Support Services**
- Expand access to **mental health hotlines and counseling centers**, particularly in high-risk regions.  
- Integrate **data analytics** into resource planning for equitable service distribution.  

### **3. Policy & Data Collaboration**
- Encourage **data sharing between health agencies and NGOs** to develop a unified prevention framework.  
- Support **continuous data monitoring** to track progress and policy effectiveness.  

### **4. Socioeconomic Support**
- Address **economic and employment factors** contributing to suicide risk through community programs and social interventions.  

### **5. Data-Driven Research**
- Continue expanding analytical models to explore **correlations between lifestyle, income, and suicide risk factors**.
